# Page and hosted conversion measurement

Ask for the page type, the conversion action, and where it completes. A Page can promote a website or app, host a form, or start a conversation. Classify the conversion by the outcome, even when acquisition begins on the same Page.

| Destination | Measurement plan |
| --- | --- |
| Owned landing page | Follow the [website guide](web.md). Scope tags to the intended path, form success, or checkout. Verify cross-domain tracking and preserve authorized attribution when the funnel crosses domains. |
| Facebook Page or Instagram profile | Start with native Business Suite or Ads Manager insights. Use profile visits, engagement or other supported metrics when they match the brief. Website GTM, GA4, Pixel and Clarity snippets belong on an owned web property. |
| Facebook/Instagram Instant Form | Use native lead submission and the correct form, ad, and reporting source. If feedback is authorized, link the actual provider lead to the CRM and send qualified or paid outcomes through an eligible integration. |
| Google-hosted lead form | Verify the native Google-hosted submit action and approved CRM delivery. For eligible downstream qualifications or purchases, use the [Google conversion route](conversion-routes.md). |
| WhatsApp or Messenger | Trace click, conversation started, qualified lead and paid outcome separately. Use the supported messaging route and preserve genuine referral identifiers where applicable. |
| Page links to a website or mobile app | Measure Page engagement in native reports and route site or app outcomes to their own destination. Check that one purchase does not appear in multiple primary routes. |

First verify the exact Page, ad account, form and destination. Then test a form or conversation and CRM delivery with a scoped QA case. Ensure any qualified or paid feedback uses the actual event source, outcome time, stable event ID, genuine value and eligible consent. See [verification](verification.md).
